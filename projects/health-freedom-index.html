Health Freedom Index

An interactive, user-weighted health index for all 3,137 U.S. counties.


The Health Freedom Index lets you decide what "healthy" means. Instead of handing you a single fixed ranking, the app gives you a set of sliders — food environment, gyms per 10k residents, primary care and mental-health provider access, uninsured rate, air quality, extreme heat days, and more — and rebuilds the national choropleth live as you change the weights.

Every county is scored 0–100 based on its national percentile within each indicator, then combined using your weights. Click any county to open a detail panel showing its overall score, national rank, and a breakdown of its biggest strengths and gaps with the measured value alongside the percentile bar. A search box, preset weighting profiles, and a live "Top Counties For You" leaderboard make it easy to explore.

Built with: Leaflet, vanilla JavaScript, and CARTO dark basemap tiles, with county boundaries from the U.S. Census Bureau and health indicators drawn from public county-level health data. Data coverage is shown per indicator (for example, "77% of counties, 736 missing") so the weighting stays honest about gaps.

This project is a work in progress — indicators and styling are still being refined.
